Privacy Policy

Aurum Nexus — internal business application of Aurum Spirits India Limited · Last updated: 6 September 2026

Aurum Nexus is the internal ERP (enterprise resource planning) system of Aurum Spirits India Limited ("we", "us"). It is used only by our own staff. This policy explains how the WhatsApp Business messaging feature of Aurum Nexus handles information.

1. What information we collect

When you send a WhatsApp message to our WhatsApp Business number, we receive from Meta (WhatsApp) your phone number, the name shown on your WhatsApp profile, the content of your message (text, images, documents, voice notes or other attachments) and the time it was sent. When we reply, we keep a copy of our reply. We do not collect any other data from your WhatsApp account and we do not access your contacts, location or device.

2. How we use it

We use this information solely to read and reply to your messages, to keep a record of our business conversations with distributors, vendors, retailers and customers, and to link a conversation to your account with us where one exists. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

3. Where it is stored

Message text and conversation records are stored in our secured cloud database (Supabase). Attachments such as photos and documents, and monthly conversation archives, are stored in a private Google Drive folder owned by the company. Access is limited to authorised staff members who have been given permission inside Aurum Nexus.

4. Sharing

Messages are transmitted through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., which processes them under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. Apart from Meta, Supabase (database hosting) and Google (file storage), we do not share your information with anyone unless required by law.

5. Retention

Conversations are kept for as long as they are needed for our business records. You may ask us at any time to delete the conversation history associated with your phone number and we will do so unless we are required by law to retain it.

6. Your choices

You can stop messaging our WhatsApp Business number at any time, or block the number in WhatsApp. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the details below.
